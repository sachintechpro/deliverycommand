    METHOD & TRANSPARENCY

    Project recovery methodology

    01Governance

    Separate facts from unverified claims, document accountability, and escalate decisions—not blame.

    02Scheduling

    Use a dependency network with earliest start / finish and a critical-path late pass.

    03Financial control

    Compare estimate at completion to the authorized budget, including intervention costs.

    04Risk integrity

    Protect mandatory quality criteria and require approval for scope or risk exceptions.

    This simulator uses fictional scenario data and deterministic assumptions, available in the source code. Results require independent validation and formal approval before use in an actual program.
